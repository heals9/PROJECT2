Do More Home Runs Mean More Wins?

By Mark C. Healey

In 2026, the MLB-best Milwaukee Brewers (108 wins) won their first World Series in 57 years despite ranking 28th in home runs. Their opponent, the Tampa Bay Rays, led the AL with 98 wins while ranking 22nd in home runs.

When asked if hitting more regular-season runs drives wins, MLB Senior Data Architect Tom Tango said,

“Ask the reverse,” he countered. “Does a team ALLOWING more HR equal more regular season wins? Of course not. To put it more plainly: does a team that hits more HR than it allows win more games than it loses? The answer should be obvious (on average, obviously not ALWAYS, because of confounding factors).

StatMuse.com data confirms a strong correlation between home runs and wins. Since 2022, the top home run-hitting teams, the Yankees, Braves, and Dodgers, have also won the most games.
